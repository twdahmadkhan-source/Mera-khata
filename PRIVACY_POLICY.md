# Privacy Policy for Mera Khata

**Effective Date:** November 2026

**Mera Khata** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Local Data Storage
All your customer names, phone numbers, photos, and ledger entries (Udhar / Jama) are stored locally on your mobile device. We do not sell or share your personal ledger data with any third party.

## 2. Optional Cloud Backup
If you choose to sign in with your Google Account and use the Cloud Backup feature, your ledger backup is stored securely in your private Firebase/Google account space so you can restore it if you change your phone.

## 3. Permissions Used
- **Camera & Photos:** Used only when you choose to attach a customer's photo.
- **Phone & WhatsApp:** Used only when you tap Call or WhatsApp to contact a customer or send a payment reminder.

## 4. Advertisements & Subscriptions
The free version of Mera Khata displays limited daily advertisements powered by Google AdMob. You can remove ads anytime by subscribing to the optional Pro plan via Google Play Billing.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via our GitHub repository: https://github.com
